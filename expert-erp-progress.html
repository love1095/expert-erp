What the ERP does today

Sales

  • Sales and purchase invoices with payments
  • A reliable order lifecycle: part shipments, cancellations and returns
  • Customers in the Cin7 layout, imported from Cin7
  • Quote, order, ship, invoice and payment, with VAT, backorders and PDFs
  • Price tiers, contract prices, credit control, statements, addresses
  • Smaller fixes before cutover: credit hold, bill numbers, costs, roles, PDFs

Purchasing

  • Purchase orders, receiving, supplier bills, reorder and FIFO stock costs

Inventory

  • Products, warehouses and live stock on hand
  • Transfers, stock history, low-stock alerts, suppliers, first purchase and sale orders
  • Real opening stock loaded from Cin7
  • Safety certificates per SKU, kits, units of measure, images, landed cost, product lifecycle

Warehouse

  • Bins, pick lists, packing, phone scanner, stock takes
  • Courier booking, labels and tracking (ready; needs the courier accounts)

Finance

  • Sales and purchase ledgers, VAT return, profit reports, Xero CSV export
  • The ERP's own accounting like Xero: ledger, P&L, balance sheet, bank, MTD VAT

Reports

  • Dashboard: stock, orders and sales on one screen
  • Dashboard widgets from the specification

Integrations

  • Shopify: orders in; fulfilment, refunds, prices and stock back out
  • Xero link through its API (ready; needs the Xero account)
  • E-mail documents, statements and reminders (ready; needs the mail account)

Platform

  • Audit log, user roles, safe stock updates when many people work at once
  • One system for many companies: each subscription has its own users and data
  • Automatic tests run on every change
  • Roles, search, reports, document templates, two-step sign-in
  • Cutover tools: import open orders, go-live check, backups
